# Original retirement policy comparison

The queued retirement admission consumer compares the frozen family with the
original collector's privately issued Source lease and Global preparation
scopes before requesting a fresh acknowledgement. The collector retains those
actual owners; a copied or decoded stored inventory cannot enter this route.

The Source comparison preserves the complete source, lease, registration, root,
audit, pager and native MAIN/WAL/SHM facts. Only the local preparation capture
nonce changes between the frozen observation and the new original preparation.
The Global comparison requires the typed initial-forward policy, exact frozen
operation/work/family digest, original registration and worker generation,
preparation nonce, acquisition and entered component ceilings. Native object
identities, devices, units and lengths must agree with the original allocation.

Each SourceLeaseMaintenance, Draining and Retired work gets a distinct MAIN
requirement and a WAL/SHM requirement. Every component remains explicit and
unit-aligned; sums are checked. Each of the two sequential Global works gets
its own complete original component budget. Concurrent and compensation work
cannot reuse those budgets. The frozen capacity plan and ordinary claims must
match the complete four allocation-class totals exactly. Neither splitting an
aggregate nor deriving future write ceilings from historical lengths is valid.

A foreign immutable command digest cannot consume or cancel another original
preparation. A matching original command with an invalid policy resolves
NoAttempt and requests reverse-prefix cleanup without a new acknowledgement,
operation identity, capacity lease or journal SQL. A matching policy can reach
the fresh original-owner acknowledgement fence, then the existing physical
family refusal.

## Remaining gates

This is logical original-policy comparison, not physical-family qualification.
The typed captured requirements and a successful acknowledgement do not grant
Start, a participant permit, effects, retries, refunds or settlement.

No durable inventory header is minted from a constant or invented journal
ownership generation. OPT-2136 still requires its genuine positive header issuer,
complete current registration/joint authority, qualified physical directions,
atomic nonce/intent/ordinary-claim/six-slice inventory persistence and unknown
readback/restart custody. OPT-2194 and OPT-2168 remain open. Historical V1/V2
and explicit tag3 storage semantics are unchanged.

The Linux acceptance fixture uses real Source/Global/journal owners and freezes
ceilings obtained from the actual producers. It verifies exact logical matching
and refusal/cleanup. It does not qualify physical capacity or retirement effects.
The component arithmetic fixtures exercise comparison only.
